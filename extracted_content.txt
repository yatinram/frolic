Sight words are words that children learn to recognise
by sight, rather than sounding them out letter by letter.
These words appear most frequently in reading
materials. Learning sight words improves reading
speed and comprehension too.
A phoneme is the smallest unit of sound in a language,
while a grapheme is the smallest unit in a writing
system that represents a sound—this can be a letter or
a group of letters. In simple terms, phonemes are the
sounds we make when we speak, and graphemes are
the symbols we use to write down those sounds.
Teaching children about phonemes and graphemes is
crucial for several reasons:
1. Foundations for Reading and Writing  
2. Spelling and Pronunciation
3. Language Awareness and Cognitive Skills
4. Early Intervention and Support

Beneﬁts of our “English Lab”
1. Language Development: Our English lab application and workbooks allow Indian
English medium school children to learn English in a more interactive and contextual way,
aiding in their language development. They are exposed to authentic English language usage
through videos, audio clips, and interactive activities, which can improve their speaking,
listening, and comprehension skills.
2. Active Engagement: Traditional learning by rote often promotes passive learning, where
children simply repeat information without truly understanding it. On the other hand, our
interactive English lab application encourages students’ active engagement and participation.
Students can interact with the content, solve problems, and explore different concepts, which
enhances their critical thinking and problem-solving abilities.
3. Multi-sensory Learning: Our English Lab course leverages creative satisfaction and sense
of self achievement. The multimedia features, such as colourful visuals, animations with sound
effects, providing a multi-sensory learning experience for children. This approach caters to
different learning styles and helps children with a different mother tongue than English to
understand and retain information better.
4. Personalised Learning: 
®
With English Lab App and “Pabulum Workbooks, children can
progress at their own pace and receive personalised feedback. They can repeat activities and
lessons until they grasp the concepts, which is not possible in a traditional rote learning setup.
This individualised learning experience enables non-English mother tongue speaking children to
build their English language skills gradually and conﬁdently.
5. Real-world Connections: Experiential learning exposes children to real-world scenarios and
practical experiences. They can engage in hands-on activities, experiments, and role plays to
understand concepts better. This approach not only enhances their understanding of English but
also provides a holistic learning experience, where they can connect English language skills to
real-life situations.
6. Motivation and Interest: Our English Lab platform incorporate level wise interactive exercises,
such as basic, intermediate and advanced along with progress tracking, which can motivate children
to actively participate in their learning process. Our English Lab also sparks curiosity and interest
through interactive and immersive experiences, making learning more enjoyable and engaging for
non-English speaking kids.
In summary, Our English Lab platform with interactive exercises offer several advantages over traditional rote
learning for non-English speaking Indian English medium children. They enhance language development,
active engagement, multi-sensory learning, personalised learning, real-world connections, motivation, and
cultural awareness, ultimately providing a more effective and fulﬁlling learning experience.

Detailed Syllabus Maths Lab: 
Following is the syllabus Frolic Math Lab for Jr kindergarten to class 5 students.
Echelon 01: Numerical counting & calculations
Module 01 : Numbers & digits - 01 digit, addition/subtraction - 01 digit. Numbers & decimal system - 02 digits,
                     addition/subtraction - 02 digits, calculations with abacus. (2 workbooks)
Module 02 : Forward and backward counting - up to 03 digits (Including skip counting), addition/subtraction with
                     abacus and mental calculations up to 3 digits. Introducing the concept of multiplication and division
                     as repeated addition and subtraction. Multiplication tables. Complex addition-subtraction &
                     grouping up to 03 digits with abacus. (3 Workbooks)
Module 04: Place value and ordering numbers - 4 & 5 digits, Division - simple and with remainders. Introduction of
                     fraction. Proper and improper fraction. Comparing and ordering fractions. (2 workbooks)
Module 05: Formation of numbers and comparing them up to 100000. Larger multiplication and  division.
                    Introduction of decimal fractions & mixed fractions. Addition and subtraction of fractions and decimal
                    fractions. (2 workbooks)  
Echelon 02: Numerical Applications - (Includes pragmatic problems and projects)
Module 01: Understanding and measuring Time, length, weight and capacity. Understanding the concept of money
                    and transactions. (2 workbooks & 1 project book)
Module 02: Understanding ratio and proportion. Understanding the concept of percentage. Data handling and
                    representing data through graphs. (2 workbook & 1 project book)  
     
Echelon 03: Geometry - (Includes pragmatic problems and projects)
Module 01: Identifying and classifying 2D and 3D shapes. Understanding angles, lines and basic geometrical
                    concepts. Measuring area, perimeter and solving pragmatic problems. (2 workbooks & 1 project book)
